Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is indoor digital billboard advertising?
LyneSign places digital screens inside local businesses and displays full-motion advertisements alongside relevant content like weather, news, and entertainment.
Where are LyneSign screens located?
LyneSign operates screens across Texas and offers location-based advertising, including opportunities in Houston.
How often does my advertisement play?
15-second advertisements can play approximately once every 10–15 minutes, depending on the selected package.
Can I target specific areas?
Yes. LyneSign supports targeting by ZIP code, DMA, city, state, or a customized geographic area.
Can I target specific audiences?
Yes. Audience targeting can consider demographics, lifestyles, shopping behaviors, interests, habits, and business categories.
Can LyneSign help create my advertisement?
Yes. LyneSign offers design services as part of its multichannel solutions.
