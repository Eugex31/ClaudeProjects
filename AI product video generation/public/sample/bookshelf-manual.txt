Step 1: Lay out the bottom panel and dowels.
Place the bottom panel on a soft surface and push the eight wooden dowels into the pre-drilled holes.

Step 2: Attach the left side.
Slide the left side panel onto the dowels until it sits flush with the bottom panel.

Step 3: Attach the right side.
Repeat on the right: line up the holes and press the right side panel onto the dowels.

Step 4: Add the top panel.
Lower the top panel onto the upper dowels so both sides are held in place.
WARNING: Two people recommended to keep the frame steady.

Step 5: Lock the joints.
Insert the eight cam locks and turn each one clockwise about half a turn until the joint is tight.
WARNING: Do not overtighten the cam locks.

Step 6: Place the shelves.
Rest the three shelves on the shelf pins, starting with the lowest one.

Step 7: Fix the back panel.
Lay the back panel on the rear of the frame and nail it along the edges.
WARNING: Anchor the bookshelf to the wall to prevent tipping.
